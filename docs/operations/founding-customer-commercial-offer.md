# Founding Customer Commercial Offer

Launch scope is apartments in Prishtina and Fushë Kosovë for owners abroad or
absent for significant periods. House inquiries may be recorded but are not
eligible for launch service.

| Package | 6-month term | 12-month term | Scheduled visits | Core boundary |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Essential Check | €450 (≈ €75/mo) | €840 (≈ €70/mo) | 1 per month | Visible-condition, access/readiness check, short update |
| Care Plus | €720 (≈ €120/mo) | €1,320 (≈ €110/mo) | 2 per month | Essential scope, issue flagging, bounded local follow-up |
| Arrival Ready | €990 (≈ €165/mo) | €1,890 (≈ €157.50/mo) | 2 per month + Home Refresh | Care Plus scope plus bounded pre-arrival readiness support |

Arrival Ready includes:

- 6-month term: 1 Home Refresh during the term
- 12-month term: 2 Home Refreshes during the term

A Home Refresh is an additional physical visit (does not replace a normal visit).
It includes vacuuming, normal dust removal, basic accessible surface wipe, basic
kitchen and bathroom surface reset, airing/ventilation, visible readiness check,
doors/windows/access check, and a final readiness update. It is explicitly not
deep cleaning.

Packages are sold as committed terms: 6 or 12 months. No month-to-month public
package is offered.

The first three paying eligible customers may receive founding status: included
setup/property-needs consultation, founder-led onboarding, and package-price
protection covering the first 12 months of the relationship. A founding customer
who initially chooses a 6-month term may renew for one additional 6-month term at
the same 6-month price; this renewal is protected under the 12-month founding
price guarantee. A founding customer who chooses a 12-month term is protected for
the full initial term. Founding status never means unlimited support. Renewal
after the protected period follows the then-current price unless agreed otherwise
in writing.

The application prevents more than three active founding proposals from
reserving a place. An expired, rejected, or superseded proposal releases its
place. Before service begins, the operator must confirm that the customer is
eligible and is among the first three who actually record payment.

Package prices exclude contractor charges, parts/materials, separately agreed
deep cleaning, major repairs, unlimited errands or coordination, rental
management, and guaranteed 24/7 emergency response.

Normal approval limit is €100. Emergency limit is €300 when the customer cannot
be reached after reasonable attempts. Both remain editable per proposal/customer.
They never authorize structural alteration, disposal of property, or
discretionary non-emergency work without explicit approval.

Public wording: "Packages from €450 for 6 months or €840 for 12 months. The
right setup depends on the visit frequency and support your apartment needs."
