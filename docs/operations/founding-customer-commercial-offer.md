# Founding Customer Commercial Offer

Launch scope is apartments in Prishtina and Fushë Kosovë for owners abroad or
absent for significant periods. House inquiries may be recorded but are not
eligible for launch service.

| Package | Monthly price | Scheduled visits | Core boundary |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Essential Check | €75 | 1 | Visible-condition, access/readiness check, short update |
| Care Plus | €110 | 2 | Checks and updates, issue flagging, bounded local follow-up |
| Arrival Ready | €160 | 2 | Care Plus scope plus bounded pre-arrival readiness support |

The first three paying eligible customers may receive founding status: included
setup/property-needs consultation, founder-led onboarding, and the package price
fixed for the first 12 months. Founding status never means unlimited support.
Renewal after month 12 follows the then-current price unless agreed otherwise in
writing.

Package prices exclude contractor charges, parts/materials, separately agreed
deep cleaning, major repairs, unlimited errands or coordination, rental
management, and guaranteed 24/7 emergency response.

Normal approval limit is €100. Emergency limit is €300 when the customer cannot
be reached after reasonable attempts. Both remain editable per proposal/customer.
They never authorize structural alteration, disposal of property, or
discretionary non-emergency work without explicit approval.

Public wording: “Packages start from €75 per month. The right setup depends on the
visit frequency and support your apartment needs.”

Legal status: commercial baseline approved by the Founder; customer-facing legal
terms and privacy wording still require separate review.
