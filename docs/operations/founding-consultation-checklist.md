# Founding Consultation Checklist

Format: approximately 30 minutes, WhatsApp voice or video, led by Founder Milot
Berisha. A consultation does not activate service.

1. Confirm apartment eligibility and Prishtina/Fushë Kosovë service area.
2. Confirm owner, location, apartment count, occupancy, and current condition.
3. Record access and key situation.
4. Capture primary concerns, maintenance issues, and desired visit frequency.
5. Capture arrival-readiness needs and communication preference.
6. Explain package inclusions, exclusions, third-party costs, and bounded support.
7. Confirm editable normal (€100 default) and emergency (€300 default) limits.
8. Recommend Essential Check, Care Plus, or Arrival Ready.
9. Record outcome, next action, follow-up date, and completion time.
10. If eligible, explain founding-customer cap, 12-month price lock, and renewal.

Do not promise structural work, disposal, unlimited coordination, guaranteed
emergency response, or service activation during the call.
